## Description

The DJI Mic Mini is a compact, ultralight wireless microphone system designed for clear and reliable audio capture\. It features a transmitter weighing only 10g, offering discreet and comfortable wearing options like magnetic attachment and clip\-on\. The system supports omnidirectional audio recording, with a receiver capable of pairing with two transmitters simultaneously\. It boasts up to 400 meters of stable audio transmission, a 48\-hour operation time when paired with a charging case, and direct Bluetooth connection capabilities with compatible DJI Osmo products\.

## Description

Real 1080P 30FPS Capture Card: UGREEN HDMI Capture Card supports input max resolution up to 4K@30Hz, output max resolution up to 1080P@30Hz\. This Capture Card connects cameras, camcorders, and other audio and video signal sources via HDMI interface, and captures HDMI signals to record to USB A or USB C devices\. It is great for you to record videos & audio clearly at any time for streaming

2\-in\-1 USB\-A & USB\-C Interface Design: The streaming capture card is for most current USB\-C and USB\-A laptops, PCs, tablets, iPads, cell phones, and other devices\. The phone/tablet can be used as a portable screen to play console games or can be used as a camera monitor\. Note: PS4 and PS5 support this capture card when HDCP is turned off

Plug and Play: As a portable plug\-and\-play streaming capture card, there is no need to install any driver and provide an external power supply, just connect and start reproducing video in HD\. Weighing only 35g, this 4k capture card is lightweight and portable, so you can slip it into your bag in case of emergency, making it perfect for live outdoor broadcasts

Ultra Low Latency Screen Sharing: The capture card uses an intelligent chip with a transmission rate of up to 480Mbps, low latency, and no delay\. It supports easily recording Nintendo Switch/Switch 2/PS4/PS5/Xbox X series videos and streaming them to streaming platforms with iPad/laptop/PC, allowing you to enjoy amazing ultra\-low latency, avoiding blue screen and latency, and no picture tearing and flickering

Wide Compatibility: Note: iPadOS devices need to be updated to 17/18 or higher to use it\. This USB video capture is fit for various operating systems and software, compatible with Windows 8\.1/10, Linux, and Android\. The video capture card is suitable for live streaming, recording, editing, and transferring video in high resolution on OBS, XSplit, Potplayer, QuickTime Player, USB Camera Viewer, and more\. The iPhone is currently not supported because the iPhone has no UVC function
